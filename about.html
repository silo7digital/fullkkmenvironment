About Kelly

Kelly K is a husband, father, pastor, author, and evangelist.

Faith and music

Kelly Kopp grew up in a Christian home and remembers believing in Jesus from an early age. As a teenager, he felt called to preach, but he wanted to play music and go on tour. After high school, he pursued a career in rock and eventually found work as a tour manager.

In interviews, Kelly has described how much he wanted to be known. He hoped that an audience would give him the sense of belonging he was looking for. Touring gave him a career he had wanted for years, but he says it left that deeper need unresolved.

The crash outside Nashville

In 2007, Kelly was managing the Christian rock band RED on its End of Silence tour. After two shows in North Carolina, the group drove back toward Nashville in a passenger van. Kelly was asleep in the back when the van hit a guardrail. The impact tore open the side of the van and threw him onto the highway.

Kelly spent about three months in and out of burn centers. Getting comfortable riding in a vehicle again took considerably longer, and the crash brought his touring career to a halt. He describes it as the first step in a longer return to faith.

Coming back to faith

After touring, Kelly worked in television and tried comedy. Years after the crash, while driving to pitch a TV project, he recalls sensing God tell him there was still a plan for his life. He had tried to return to church before, and he was wary of making another promise he might break. In his telling, that conversation was the point when he asked God to change his heart.

He began reading Scripture, listening to sermons, and going back to church with his family. The desire to preach, which he remembered from his teenage years, returned. Getting started took time. He recalls contacting about a hundred churches around Tulsa to offer to speak and receiving no replies.

From a phone camera to the pulpit

Kelly started writing online before trying short videos. He remembers filming one of his first teachings with his phone in his hand and seeing it reach 92 people. That was enough to make him want to record another one. In 2016, a video on whether getting a tattoo is a sin reached a much larger audience. Kelly says invitations to speak at churches, conferences, and camps followed.

That same approach carries into his ministry today. Kelly teaches Scripture on social media and in livestreamed Bible studies, preaches at churches and events, and writes books for readers who want to go deeper. His books include Reckless Love Revolution and Think About That for a Minute, a 40-day devotional. He and his wife, Lindsay, serve as staff pastors at Limitless Church in Oklahoma, according to his speaker bio.

Make Jesus known

Kelly K Ministries exists to share the Gospel and help people put their faith into practice. In his teaching, Kelly returns to forgiveness, repentance, and the peace and freedom he believes people can find in Jesus. His own story is one of the ways he explains why that message matters to him.
