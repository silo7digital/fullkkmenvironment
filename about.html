About Kelly

Kelly K is a husband, father, pastor, author, and evangelist.

Life on the road

Before entering ministry, Kelly worked as a musician and managed tours for major rock bands. Music and touring were his work and the reason he spent so much time on the road.

A turning point

While out on tour, Kelly survived a car crash that nearly took his life. He describes the crash as a turning point. He left his former career behind and turned to Jesus. In time, he returned to traveling, this time to preach and teach.

Make Jesus known

Today, Kelly shares the Gospel through Bible studies, books, social media, and speaking at churches and events. His teaching covers forgiveness, repentance, and salvation, with the aim of helping people experience the freedom, peace, and joy he believes Scripture promises in Jesus.

Kelly also encourages believers to put their faith into practice and share the Gospel with others.
